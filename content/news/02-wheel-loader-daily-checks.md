Most breakdowns give a warning first. A short walk-around at the start of each shift is the cheapest maintenance there is.

## Walk around the machine

- Look under the machine for fresh oil, fuel or coolant on the ground.
- Check tyres for cuts, bulges and pressure.
- Look at hydraulic hoses and fittings for wear, rubbing or leaks.
- Check the bucket teeth and cutting edge.

## Check the fluids

- Engine oil, coolant and hydraulic oil at the right level, using the sight glasses and dipsticks.
- Fuel and, where fitted, AdBlue.
- Drain water from the fuel water separator if the indicator shows it.

## In the cab

- Seat belt, mirrors, lights, horn and reverse alarm working.
- No warning lights after start up.
- Brakes and steering responding normally.

Write down anything unusual and tell your supervisor. If something looks wrong, call our service desk before it gets worse.
