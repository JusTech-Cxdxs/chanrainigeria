Electric forklifts with lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries are clean, quiet and need far less upkeep than older lead acid trucks. A few good habits help the battery give its full working life.

## Charging

- Use the charger supplied with the truck.
- Opportunity charging during breaks is fine with LFP batteries. There is no need to wait until the battery is empty.
- Keep chargers in a dry, ventilated area away from direct sun and rain.

## Daily use

- Avoid leaving the truck at a very low charge for long periods.
- Keep the battery compartment clean and dry.
- Report any warning on the display to your supervisor straight away.

## Planned maintenance

Request planned maintenance at the intervals in the operator's manual. Our technicians check the battery management system, cables, connectors and charger as part of each visit.
