Compressed natural gas stations need careful planning. Having the right information ready helps us recommend the right equipment and plan the installation.

## Information we will ask for

- **Site location and layout**, with photos or a drawing showing access for vehicles.
- **Gas supply**, including the pipeline pressure available at the site, or whether the station will be supplied by gas trailers.
- **Expected demand**, such as the number and type of vehicles to be filled each day and the busiest hours.
- **Power supply**, including the grid connection and any backup generation.

## The equipment

A typical station brings together a compressor, storage, dispensers and the safety and control systems that link them. The right sizes depend on your supply pressure and the demand you expect.
