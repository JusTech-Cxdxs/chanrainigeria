When a machine stops on site, every hour counts. These simple steps help our technician start work as soon as they arrive.

## Before the visit

- **Share the exact location.** A Google Maps pin is best, plus the name of a contact person at the gate.
- **Send the serial number and hour meter reading.** Both are on the data plate and the dashboard. They tell us which parts and service records to bring.
- **Describe what happened.** Warning lights, error codes, noises or leaks, and when it started.
- **Take two or three photos.** The fault, the data plate and the area around the machine.

## On the day

- Park the machine on firm, level ground where it is safe to work, away from moving traffic.
- Lower attachments to the ground and switch off the engine.
- Make sure someone who knows the machine is available to answer questions.

## After the visit

You receive a service record listing what was found, what was done and when the next service is due. Keep it with the machine's papers.
