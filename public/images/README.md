Tips for replacement photos:

- The site places a dark gradient and white headline text over banners and hero slides. Choose photos where the left and centre-left area is fairly calm (sky, wall, soft background) and the main subject sits on the right.
- Export as JPG at about 80% quality and aim for under 500 KB per file.
- Category images also appear as cards on the home page, so keep the main subject near the centre.
